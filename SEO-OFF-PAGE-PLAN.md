# AMREN Fresh — Off-Page SEO Plan

Off-page SEO cannot be implemented through frontend code. This document is the
working plan for building genuine, relevant authority and local signals for
**AMREN Fresh** (`https://fresh.amren.ae/`).

Principles that apply to every activity in this document:

- Keep **NAP** (Name, Address, Phone) information **100% consistent** across
  every listing, profile and mention.
- Never create fake profiles, fake reviews or fake business mentions.
- Never buy spam backlinks, use PBNs, or use automated backlink software.
- Never mass-submit to low-quality directories.
- Prioritize **relevance** (UAE, food, wholesale, B2B) and **authority** over
  volume.
- Placeholders below (address, phone, email, socials) must be confirmed with
  the business before publishing anywhere — do not invent them.
